Food Donation Project
This project aims to create a web-based platform that connects individuals or organizations who want to donate food with those in need. The goal is to tackle food insecurity while reducing food waste by facilitating the redistribution of surplus food to people or communities that could benefit from it.

Overview
The Food Donation Project is designed to bridge the gap between food surplus and food insecurity. It enables food donors (individuals, grocery stores, restaurants, etc.) to post food donations and allows people or organizations in need of food to request it. By connecting these two groups, we can ensure that excess food is utilized in a way that helps those who need it the most.

Features
User Authentication: Secure login and registration for both donors and recipients.
Donation Listings: Donors can create posts listing available food items.
Request System: Recipients can request food based on their needs.
Search & Filter: Users can search and filter available food by location, type, and quantity.
Messaging System: Donors and recipients can communicate for coordination.
Admin Dashboard: Allows admins to manage users, donations, and oversee platform operations.
Donation History: Users can track past donations and requests for transparency and accountability.
Notifications: Email and/or SMS notifications for new donations, requests, and updates.
